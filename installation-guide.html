Installation Guide

Step-by-step instructions to install your BUECON hardware correctly and beautifully.

Designed for easy installation

Every BUECON product is engineered for straightforward installation. Our concealed-fixing system gives your bathroom a clean, seamless look. Follow this universal guide for most of our hardware ranges.

Step-by-Step Process

1

Unbox & Verify Contents

Carefully remove all components from the packaging. Verify that all parts listed in the included parts sheet are present — this typically includes the main fixture, concealed mounting plate, wall plugs, screws, and an Allen key. Contact us if anything is missing before proceeding.

Tip: Keep the packaging until installation is complete in case you need to re-check component details.
2

Choose & Mark Mounting Position

Determine the ideal height and position for your fixture. Use standard bathroom ergonomic guidelines: towel bars at 48–52 inches from floor, robe hooks at 65–70 inches, toilet paper holders at 26 inches from floor and 8–12 inches from the toilet. Mark the screw hole positions lightly with a pencil.

Tip: Always use a spirit level when marking multiple fixing points to ensure perfectly aligned installation.
3

Check for Pipes & Wiring

Before drilling, use a pipe and wire detector to verify there are no concealed utilities behind the marked area. This step is critical for safety. If drilling into tiles, use a tile drill bit at slow speed to avoid cracking.

Tip: Apply masking tape over the drill mark on tiles — this reduces vibration and prevents the bit from slipping.
4

Drill & Insert Wall Plugs

Drill holes at your marked positions to the appropriate depth for your wall plugs (typically 30–40mm). Clean out any dust from the holes. Insert the provided wall plugs by hand, tapping gently with a hammer until flush with the wall surface.

5

Fix the Concealed Mounting Plate

Align the mounting bracket or back plate over the wall plug holes. Insert the provided screws and tighten firmly with a screwdriver. Do not overtighten — firm is sufficient. Verify the plate is level before final tightening.

6

Attach the Fixture

Slide or click the main fixture body onto the mounted bracket according to your model's specific mechanism. Most BUECON fixtures feature a click-lock system. Once engaged, use the provided Allen key to tighten the concealed set screw — this locks the fixture securely in place.

Tip: Do not apply excessive force. A firm quarter-turn past finger-tight is sufficient to secure most fixtures.
7

Final Check & Clean

Test the fixture by applying gentle downward and outward pressure to confirm secure mounting. Wipe down all surfaces with a soft, dry cloth to remove any fingerprints, dust, or installation residue. Your BUECON hardware is now ready for use.

Tip: Avoid abrasive cleaners during and after installation. A mild soap solution and microfibre cloth keeps BUECON finishes pristine for years.

SALT Series — Installation Notes

SALT Series features a satin stainless steel finish that requires extra care during installation. Avoid contact with silicone sealants containing acetic acid — use neutral-cure silicone where sealing is required near the fixture. The SALT Series uses M6 concealed fixings. Torque the set screw to 3–4 Nm maximum. For the SALT towel rack (dual-rail model), ensure both end brackets are installed at identical heights — a 1mm variance is acceptable, beyond that will cause visible misalignment.
