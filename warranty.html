Structural Integrity
The core structural integrity of all BUECON stainless steel and brass components is guaranteed against structural failure for a full decade from date of purchase.

✓ What's Covered

  • Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
  • Structural failure of metal components under normal use loads
  • Premature finish degradation (peeling, flaking, excessive tarnishing)
  • Faulty or defective concealed fixing mechanisms
  • Corrosion of stainless steel in normal humid bathroom environments
  • Defective welds, joints, or assembly failures
  • Glass components — breakage due to material defect (not impact)

✗ Exclusions

  • Damage caused by improper installation or failure to follow installation guide
  • Normal wear, scratches, or minor surface marks from daily use
  • Damage from abrasive cleaners, harsh chemicals, or bleach
  • Accidental damage — impact, breakage, or misuse
  • Finish damage caused by exposure to salt water or pool chemicals
  • Products purchased from unauthorised resellers
  • Damage resulting from modification or non-approved accessories

How to make a warranty claim

1

Contact Us

Reach out via our contact form or email with your order details and a description of the issue.

2

Send Photos

Share clear photos of the defect along with your proof of purchase (invoice or order number).

3

Assessment

Our quality team reviews your claim within 3 business days and confirms whether it falls under warranty.

4

Resolution

We'll arrange a replacement, repair, or refund — whichever is most appropriate for your situation.

Warranty Terms & Conditions

This warranty applies to products purchased directly from BUECON or authorised BUECON distributors and dealers. The warranty period begins on the date of original purchase. Warranty is non-transferable and applies to the original purchaser only. BUECON's liability under this warranty is limited to repair, replacement, or refund at BUECON's sole discretion. This warranty does not cover incidental or consequential damages. Warranty claims must be submitted within the applicable warranty period. Products must be installed in accordance with BUECON's installation guidelines. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights which vary by jurisdiction. For commercial and hospitality installations, separate commercial warranty terms may apply — please contact our trade team for details.
